## What this app is

**Organon Reader** presents the *Organon of Medicine* in four English editions — Wheeler's translation of the first edition (1913), Devrient's of the fourth (1833), Dudgeon's of the fifth (1849) and Boericke's of the sixth (1922) — searchable and readable offline.

It is a **reference and reading tool**. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, prescribe, treat or cure any condition.

## Not medical advice

Every rubric, remedy, keynote, relationship or passage shown here is **quoted from a historical text**, not a recommendation. A remedy suggested by a repertory is a hypothesis for a registered practitioner to test against the individual patient — it is not a prescription, and it is not advice to you.

No doctor–patient relationship arises from using this app.

The editions are different books, and the app does not merge them. Where they word a section differently, each reading is available; none is presented as the better one.

## Sources and their limits

First edition: *Organon of the Rational Art of Healing*, 1810, translated by C. E. Wheeler, 1913 — public domain. Third-party sources omit Hahnemann's Introduction and most of his footnotes, and so does this app.

Fourth edition: *Organon of the Healing Art*, 1829, translated by Charles H. Devrient, 1833 — public domain. Paragraph numbers lost in the scanned printing are named as missing, never filled.

Sixth edition: *Organon of Medicine*, completed 1842, translated by William Boericke, 1922 — public domain.

Fifth edition: *Organon of Medicine*, 1833, translated by R. E. Dudgeon, 1849 — public domain.

Where classical authors disagree, the disagreement is preserved. It is not resolved here. Homeopathic literature reflects the understanding of its own time and is not a statement of modern clinical evidence.

The reading text comes from the Médi-T transcription (homeoint.org) and is corroborated section by section against printed scans; `tools/extract.py` refuses text that fails that test. Transcription can still be wrong. Where the app and the printed page differ, the printed page is right.

## If you are unwell

Consult a registered medical practitioner. Do not start, stop or delay treatment on the basis of anything shown here.

**In an emergency, call 108 (India) or your local emergency number.**

## Responsibility

Clinical decisions remain with the qualified practitioner treating the patient. This app records and displays what its sources wrote; it does not decide anything.
