## Organon of Medicine, sixth edition

Author: Samuel Hahnemann (1755–1843).
Translation: William Boericke, 1922.
Text used: the Médi-T transcription published at homeoint.org.
Corroboration: three OCR scans of the printed book, compared section by section.
Basis for public-domain status: author and translator both died more than sixty years ago; the copyright
term under s.22 of the Copyright Act, 1957 has expired. Confirm against the edition you copy from.

## Organon of the Healing Art, fourth edition

Author: Samuel Hahnemann (1755–1843).
Translation: Charles H. Devrient, with notes by Samuel Stratten, 1833.
Text used: four OCR scans of the 1833 printing, held by the Internet Archive (`bub_gb_EnEFAAAAQAAJ`,
`homoeopathicmedi00hahn`, `homopathicmedic00devrgoog`, `b33290180`). The scans are the text; there is no
intermediate transcription. Each section records the scan it was read from and its corroboration figure.
Basis: as above.

## Organon of the Rational Art of Healing, first edition

Author: Samuel Hahnemann (1755–1843).
Translation: C. E. Wheeler, 1913 (Everyman's Library).
Text used: two OCR scans of the 1913 printing, held by the Internet Archive (`organonofrationa00hahn`,

Basis: author d. 1843; translator d. 1926; the translation was published 1913.

## The machine translations

No English translation of the second or third edition has ever been published. The English offered for those
two editions was generated by Google Translate when the bundles were built (`tools/translate-mt.py`).
It has no translator and no publisher, it is not Hahnemann's English, and it is labelled as machine
translation on every section it appears on. It is included so those editions can be read in English at all,
and it is subordinate to the German printed beside it.

## The Médi-T transcription

The Médi-T project at homeoint.org transcribed the fifth and sixth editions. The transcription is the immediate source of
the text for those two editions, even where the underlying work is out of copyright, and it is credited for that reason.
