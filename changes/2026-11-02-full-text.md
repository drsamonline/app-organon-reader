# 1.3.0 — the whole book, and the sources audited

The owner sent three sources and asked for the missing sections to be filled and the app
audited. Result: **291 of 291 sections**, each one traceable.

- **Reading text:** the MediT transcription (homeoint.org) of the 1922 Boericke
  translation, parsed sixth-edition-only (`tools/parse-homeoint.py`): where the site
  prints a fifth-edition variant beside a sixth, the fifth is skipped.
- **Proof it is the same book:** for each section, the fraction of its 12-letter
  sequences found verbatim in the three OCR scans - median **0.976**, minimum **0.729**,
  floor for acceptance **0.60**. A paraphrase scores near zero, so this is a real check.
- **Provenance on screen:** every section shows `text from the MediT transcription -
  corroborated against 3 scans (98% of its word-runs found verbatim)`.
- **Refused, with reasons:** the drcherylkasdorf.com PDF is the **Kunzli/Naude/Pendleton
  1982 translation, (c) The Hahnemann Foundation** - a different, in-copyright
  translation; `in.ernet.dli.2015.31779` is **Sarkar's edition with commentary**, a
  different book whose OCR cannot read its own marks; homeopathyworld.in drops footnotes
  and garbles the 150th aphorism. All recorded in `data/README.md`.
- **Audit of the app itself:** the suite went 26 to 27 assertions; the gap-stepping tests
  now run against a purpose-built fixture (a bundle with the 7th section removed) because
  the real bundle no longer has gaps, and a new assertion checks the corroboration floor
  is met by every single section.
