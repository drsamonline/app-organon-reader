# Organon Reader — first release (1.0.0)

Built from the app master list, entry **#132**, with the spec in
`forge/QUEUE/app-organon-reader.md` (READY).

- **The text**: *Organon of Medicine*, sixth edition, Boericke translation 1922, from
  the Internet Archive scan — public domain. 156 of the 291 sections carry a readable
  section mark; the reader publishes that number and names what is missing instead of
  renumbering.
- **Search**: phrase search over the bundled text. A term that occurs in one section
  returns that section first, with its number and the matched line. A term shorter
  than two characters is refused as too short — never reported as "no matches".
- **Bookmarks and notes**: kept on the device, verified by reading the value back; a
  write that cannot be made is reported and never shown as kept.
- **Corrupt state**: an unreadable saved file is reported, offered for export, and
  does not stop the text opening.
- **Offline**: no `fetch`, no `XMLHttpRequest`, no beacon, no socket, no off-origin
  resource. The suite asserts all four and that the bundle hashes the same twice.
