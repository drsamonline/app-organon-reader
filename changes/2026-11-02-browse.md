# 1.2.0 — browse the book, and sixth edition only

**The complaint, and it was fair:** the reader showed one section at a time with no way
to reach another except search. Now:

- the left-hand column lists **every section the bundle carries**, in order, each row
  previewing its first line; a section that no scan could read appears in place as
  "§3 — not readable in any scan", so the shape of the gap is visible rather than hidden;
- a **§ box** jumps to a number; asking for one that is not carried says so and opens the
  next section that is, never a different aphorism under the wrong number;
- **Next / Previous** buttons at the top and bottom of the text, and the **← / →** keys,
  both stepping over gaps (`step()` in `docs/reader.js`, tested);
- the current section is highlighted and scrolled into view.

**Edition discipline.** The three scans are all the 1922 Boericke translation of the
sixth edition, and that is now enforced rather than assumed: `require_sixth_edition()`
in `tools/extract.py` reads each scan's own front matter and **refuses** any file that
does not declare "SIXTH EDITION" and Boericke. The Dudgeon fifth edition and the 1913
"rational art of healing" are therefore not merely unused — they cannot be merged in.
The evidence line for each scan is carried in the bundle's `meta.scans`.

Suite: **26/26**, with new assertions for the ordered list, stepping over a gap, both
ends of the book, jumping to an uncarried number, and "every scan declares the sixth
edition in Boericke's translation".
